Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 October 2026

FactVideoMaker ("the app") is a private desktop tool that runs on its owner's own computer. It is used only by its owner to create short videos and publish them to the owner's own YouTube channels and Facebook Pages. It is not offered to the public and has no user accounts.

Information the app accesses

  • YouTube: with the owner's permission, the app uses the YouTube Data API scopes youtube.upload (to upload the owner's videos to the owner's channels) and youtube.readonly (to read the connected channel's name and ID so it can be shown in the app).
  • Facebook: with the owner's permission, the app uses a Page access token to publish the owner's videos as Reels on the owner's own Pages.

The app does not access, collect or store information about viewers, subscribers or any other people.

How information is stored

Access tokens and settings are stored only in local files on the owner's computer. They are never sent to the app developer or any third party, and are used only to talk directly to the official Google (YouTube) and Meta (Facebook) APIs.

How information is used and shared

Google user data is used only to upload videos and show the channel name inside the app. It is not sold, shared, used for advertising, or used to train AI models. FactVideoMaker's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

YouTube

By connecting a YouTube channel you also agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Removing access

The owner can disconnect a channel in the app at any time, and can revoke the app's access to a Google account at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Deleting the app's local files removes all stored tokens.
