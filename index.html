FactVideoMaker

A private desktop tool that makes short educational videos (interesting facts, history and true stories) and publishes them to its owner's own YouTube channels and Facebook Pages.

What it does

  • Writes a short script about a fact or a true, documented story.
  • Adds a computer-generated voiceover, licensed stock footage and subtitles.
  • Uploads the finished vertical video, with its title, description and hashtags, to the owner's own channels.

Who uses it

FactVideoMaker is not a public service and has no sign-up. It runs only on its owner's own computer and is used only to manage the owner's own channels: Fact Vault Daily, The Cold Files, Against All Odds Stories, The Heist Files, History Glitch Daily, Hero Tails and The Eerie Archive.

How it uses YouTube

The tool uses the YouTube Data API only to upload the owner's own videos to the owner's own channels and to read the channel name for display. It does not read, collect or share any data about viewers or other users.
